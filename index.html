FAQs

What sizes can you build?

Common footprints range 10–30m². We design to suit your site and purpose, including height rules and transport limits.

Do you handle consent?

Many cabins are exempt. If consent is required, we can coordinate plans and work with local professionals.

Can you do off‑grid?

Yes — solar, composting toilets, rainwater, and efficient heating options are available.

Who builds your cabins?

Zeke is a qualified builder and personally oversees each build from frame to finish, supported by trusted local partners.

Do you use certified trades for services?

Yes. Certified tradespeople complete any plumbing, gasfitting, and electrical work as required, with appropriate compliance documentation supplied.

How are cabins delivered and sited?

We can arrange a Hiab crane truck to place the cabin on site. We're also happy to assist with siting advice and coordination on delivery day—just let us know access details and your preferred location.

Let's chat about your cabin

Tell us a bit about your site and ideas. We'll come back with friendly advice and an indicative price.
